How to read this

Criteria: at least 1 bedroom and 1 bath, in-unit washer/dryer, AC, at least 1 parking stall (2 preferred), rent up to $4,200. Listings from $4,201 to $4,600 appear only when they add a second bedroom, a second stall, or included utilities. "All-in" adds an estimated $200 a month for utilities unless the listing says they are included. The score starts at 50 and moves with price per square foot against the area, Zillow's rent estimate, all-in cost against the target, size, bedrooms, parking, location, whether the building is comparable to Aalii, and whether it is furnished; open "Why this score" on any card for the breakdown. "Check" marks something the listing does not state, so confirm it with the landlord. Sources: Zillow (ok), Craigslist (ok).
